# Frozen scope compliance matrix

## Assessment

**The working tree does not comply with the frozen scope as a complete operator workflow.** Several controls are visible in source, including a human approval gate for scheduled renewal mail, bounded refresh, and the disabled JoFotara submission controller. Missing migration and UI work prevent an end-to-end claim. The follow-up also found an enabled recovery path for historical AI routing decisions and a payment prerequisite still enforced by the renewal UI.

This assessment compares `docs/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SCOPE.md` §§1–29 with the working tree on `main` at `32013c24488b6ea68c7e886a779f0801727aae54`. The working tree contains earlier unfinished local changes. Where HEAD differs, the matrix says so. The source hierarchy in §29 governs this assessment. The source is [CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SCOPE.md](../docs/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SCOPE.md). The original reports remain historical records. See [initial findings](CODEX_INITIAL_REPORT.md), [future vision](CODEX_FUTURE_VISION.md), [roadmap](CODEX_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md), and [coverage verification](REPOSITORY_COVERAGE_REPORT.md).

**Status:** **S** source supports the requirement on the stated path, with no runtime claim; **P** partial implementation or proof; **G** confirmed gap or conflicting code; **U** unverified or policy-dependent; **D** deliberately deferred; **C** process constraint assessed separately. Source ranges identify evidence, not executed tests. No build, test suite, database connection, migration, deployment, or request to a provider was performed for this matrix. A negative search is reported as limited evidence. **Required** means the scope demands it. Optional decisions and design proposals appear after the matrix.

**File references in the tables:** `API` paths are relative to `apps/api/src/`, `M` to `apps/api/src/modules/`, and `W` to `apps/web/`. `schema.prisma` means `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma`. Component filenames without a further directory are under `apps/web/components/`. Audit IDs retain their original meaning. **SC-01** and **SC-02** below supplement the historical findings.

- **SC-01 — confirmed AI recovery side effect, High.** The original A-13 focused on UI and stored/effective flag drift. ## Product-owner decisions still open

1. **Fulfillment prerequisites and prior cases.** §5 orders invoice/collection before fulfillment, while §10 says payment status must not block it. Must an invoice exist, must it be officially recorded, or is a prior accepted proposal sufficient? Decide how to handle historical accepted cases without an explicit interval and proposals after an out-of-band date change. Never reintroduce a payment prerequisite.
2. **Recipient editing and reply semantics.** The recipient is reviewed, but allowable edits and exceptions are unspecified. Decide whether a customer-associated address is required, who can approve exceptions, and how to treat a contact changed after queuing. Define whether unread is shared or per person and whether Keep in Review clears the badge. Decide if the optional inbound subject fallback must be active for the current cycle under other owner instructions.
3. **Session revocation semantics.** Confirm whether logout invalidates refresh immediately and lets an already-issued access token expire, or whether privileged requests must immediately fail. Confirm expected role-change UI updates and failure behavior when revocation cannot be persisted.
4. **AI diagnostics.** Administrative status/history may remain. Confirm whether model list/test controls must be hidden, blocked entirely or retained as inert configuration. No diagnostic path can reactivate operational AI. No real provider request was needed for this assessment.
5. **Manual issuer/customer and official evidence.** Clarify whether an existing customer can ever be invoiced under another issuer and by which role. Confirm source for missing tax treatment, historical QR corrections, official date display, and the minimum evidence operators record. Keep buyer fields out of the issuer tax slot and preserve all history.
6. **Payment and receipt edge cases.** Confirm whether only reconciled official invoices take payments, how overpayments/corrections/refunds are handled, and policy for currency/date changes and cancelled invoices. Define receipt numbering, duplicate copies and correction/void policy. None of these is an automatic expansion of §18 or §19.
7. **Mail retry decisions and internal UX.** Confirm what a sent date means operationally and how to handle an uncertain prior delivery. Decide final navigation labels/grouping and which fields warrant Advanced access. Optional telemetry is separate from customer messaging.
8. **Print acceptance.** The latest NUSRV reference takes priority. Agree on acceptable tolerances for multi-page and bilingual output and how Future and personal variants diverge. An earlier reference comparison cannot decide acceptance of the current code.
