## Revised release order

1. **Isolated foundations once authorized.** Design/stage settled schema and nullability, tax slot and structural validators, session-aware auth/restart, and fail-closed deferred gates. Fix existing relevant fixtures. No live calls.
2. **Safe outbox execution.** Attempt boundary and uncertainty exclusion, safe scheduling, approval provenance and recipient/hold policy. Generate/send only with explicit human intent. Add transport and reconciliation evidence with fixture coverage before enabling clarification or status claims.
3. **Human operations and finance.** Complete authorized reminder/reply UI, four review actions, case-sourced invoice creation, official evidence link, and gated fulfillment. Sequence payment-stage and receipt guards ahead of user-visible activation. Finish approved templates, manager-only action wiring and full active mail status path once gaps close. Treat applicability of existing approval as a product check, never infer it from role label.
4. **Release evidence.** Fresh/populated disposable MariaDB, full regression once at end, strict typecheck/lint/build, browser session/renewal/PDF fixtures, and mixed-version security/rollback rehearsals. Owner review before any separate authorization for push/deploy. A clearly stated mock/integration pass is not real provider acceptance.

Blockers and actor decisions are listed in [Owner Decision Gaps](OWNER_DECISION_GAPS.md). Concrete work packages and rollback gates are in [P0 Implementation Readiness](P0_IMPLEMENTATION_READINESS.md).
