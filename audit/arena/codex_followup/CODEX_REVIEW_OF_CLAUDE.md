# Independent review of Claude’s owner reconciliation

## Disposition

**Accept the twelve-decision record as the policy input. Revise the implementation specification before treating it as ready.** Claude correctly preserved the main intent, including the manual workflow, payment independence, deferred integrations, and honest mail evidence. Several proposed rules or implementation shortcuts would block an approved action, grant authority without approval, or overstate evidence.

Authority in this review is the [approved decisions](../owner_reconciliation/OWNER_DECISIONS_APPROVED.md), then the [current scope](../../../docs/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SCOPE.md), then the working implementation, prior plan and historical notes. The [signed master plan](../FINAL_MASTER_PLAN.md), [verified findings](../VERIFIED_FINDINGS.md) and [five-round verdict](../FINAL_VERDICT.md) are the engineering baseline, not newer business policy. AGENTS and PROJECT_STATUS preserve architectural and historical context. Prior instructions for automated reminders, AI, JoFotara API, shared issuer layout and paid-only fulfillment yield where the owner superseded them. The reconciliation log and empty round response show no completed live reconciliation debate.

Evidence below is from the working tree at HEAD `32013c24488b6ea68c7e886a779f0801727aae54` plus local edits. Paths prefixed **API** mean `apps/api/src/`, **M** means `apps/api/src/modules/`, and **WEB** means `apps/web/`. Conclusions labeled as risks or proposals require later tests. Prior synthetic QR evidence is historical. No current runtime, migration, SMTP/IMAP, or production proof is claimed.

## Finance details and barriers

**Official recording.** Existing manual recording requires number and date, writes MANUAL provenance and separate official amounts, and permits an accepted invoice to require reconciliation. `invoices.service.ts:529–624,925–932`. That distinction is why “reconciliation complete” cannot silently be added to OD-3. Define a purpose-specific official predicate and preserve stricter printability if already required by the existing print policy. Legacy accepted rows may need evidenced association or review. Number uniqueness is issuer-scoped. Case linking must be effective only for the same verified subscription/customer/issuer and intended cycle. Reject reusing an invoice across inconsistent cycles. Decide policy for corrections, replacements and distinct valid invoices. A dedicated provenance association is an alternative if a metadata-only FK edit on a locked document is too risky. One defensible candidate is sufficient once the owner approves candidate rules, without requiring payment data. New case-created invoices need a stable creation key with explicit cancelled/reissue handling. Update both API read model and UI from the same eligibility result.

**Payments and receipts.** A candidate design for draft-backed payment records needs an amount/currency context and a clear policy when invoices change. Existing summary switches to official amount and currency but sums all historical amounts as if they have the same currency. The signed `formatMoney` helper was written for nonnegative invoice amounts and can format a negative balance incorrectly. Source: `payments.service.ts:13–17,32–39`, `payment-money.ts:9–12`, `invoice-money.ts:24–28`. This becomes reachable if draft payment exceeds a later official balance or currency changes. Test money immutability, smaller official totals, issuer/currency mismatch and reconciliation. If no-invoice recording is approved, prefer explicit unapplied-payment identity and mandatory issuer/customer or approved counterparty and exact currency, then audited allocation. Do not make the FK nullable with no replacement business identity. Existing status choices for cancelled drafts and cleanups do not settle what happens to a linked payment. `M/customers/customers.service.ts:358–418` and `M/subscriptions/subscriptions.service.ts:296–336` delete related rows, so new linkage or evidence FKs need clear domain errors and retention tests rather than cascade deletion.

For receipt print, use approved stamp-free references and persist a narrow document snapshot rather than a broad invoice view containing raw fields, QR blobs or joined model metadata (current `payments.service.ts:45–52`). Keep the customer-facing number identical to the official invoice number, even on multiple receipts. Use receipt internal ID, issue/revision and payment amount/date to distinguish copies. Revision/void should atomically store actor, reason, time, retained documents and idempotency, lock against concurrent correction, and prevent reprinting voided versions as current. Do not turn “modify receipt” into permission to contradict amount/currency/accounting facts, and do not let deleting a document silently delete a payment. Use clear manager capability naming only after the owner maps it.

## Mail evidence and recovery

Verified source paths include `M/mail/mail-transport.ts` (void interface), `smtp-mail-transport.ts:50–64` (result discarded), `mail-outbound.service.ts:157–167,249–283,314–324,654–743` (claim, acceptance, generic requeue), and `M/communications/operator-reply-outbound.service.ts:118–130,165–197,211–221,368–443` (separate reply lease/attempt and retry). DB fields in `schema.prisma:1330–1380` model one inbound folder and cursor pair. `M/mail/imap-mailbox-reader.ts:226–246` extracts human-message fields without exposing a structured DSN. `mail-inbound-ingest.service.ts:178–233,385–435` advances INBOX cursor and persists ordinary inbound messages. Some bounces can look like customer replies if correlated. They will not **all** link to customers or renewals. Claimed behavior for any provider is untested.

| Evidence or state | Proposed handling |
| --- | --- |
| Prepared / Queued / Sending | Prepared is a draft with no SMTP attempt. A command writes immutable approval and outbox identity. A claimed lease and a durable attempt-start marker are distinct. Selection and claim share eligibility. Token and attempt identity fence persistence, not the external I/O. |
| Accepted by SMTP | Normalize the final submission outcome and verify the intended envelope recipient was accepted. Retain timestamp, attempt ID, sanitized bounded response/code and recipient, with real/mock evidence explicitly labeled. A final positive DATA completion transfers responsibility but is not proof of final delivery. [RFC 5321 §4.2.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5321.html#section-4.2.5) |
| Confirmed in Sent | Read only from the configured or unambiguously discovered folder with independent cursor by mailbox/folder/UIDVALIDITY. Match stored Message-ID plus account/recipient evidence and flag ambiguous matches. Do not mark Seen, APPEND a copy or imply provider auto-files. Bound historical backfill to relevant candidates and quarantine cursor resets. [RFC 6154 §2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6154.html#section-2) |
| Delivery confirmed, Failed/Bounced, Delayed | Parse bounded structured DSN fields including Original-Envelope-Id where supported, recipient, action/status and retained headers. Request DSN only where server supports it. Reject or quarantine unauthenticated, spoof-looking, conflicting or ambiguous evidence, and dedupe by mailbox, folder identity, UID and correlated attempt/recipient. Heuristic senders/headers are leads only. DSN success is delivery evidence, never read evidence. Do not use read receipts to upgrade trust automatically. Keep DSNs out of reply/unread and never move a renewal from an untrusted report. [RFC 3464 §2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3464.html#section-2), [Nodemailer DSN](https://nodemailer.com/message/dsn) |
| Uncertain | An unknown protocol phase, transport disconnect/timeout around completion, failed persistence after attempt, stale in-progress attempt or ownership loss can be ambiguous. Reconcile evidence before any user action. A definite nonacceptance can be separated technically only when documented adapter/stage/result evidence proves it. A bare timeout, `ECONNRESET`, or generic retry flag is not that proof. No automatic resend of uncertain customer reminders. |
| Legacy messages and conflicts | Do not invent timestamps or recipient confirmation from legacy DELIVERED. Label known historical acceptance semantics and unknown evidence explicitly. Preserve signal precedence/history, late bounce after acceptance, conflicting evidence and mixed-recipient outcomes where relevant. Status refresh must be read/evidence processing, never a hidden resend. |

**Clarification transition:** recommend authenticated evidence of final SMTP acceptance for the intended recipient as sufficient for “sent,” subject to owner selection. Do not wait forever for a Sent-folder copy or positive DSN if the owner chooses SMTP acceptance. Atomically store attempt/acceptance and advance only the matching still-pending workflow revision. A newer reply, Accept, Do Not Renew or another clarification prevents stale transition. Record a success even if it cannot advance the case. If recovery later establishes sufficient evidence, use the same guarded path. On a late verified bounce, surface failure and an action for the operator. Automatic reversal of the case would require a policy decision. Distinguish message read from decision pending, including a second reply arriving after the first was read.

**Acceptance gap:** require fake SMTP tests for partial acceptance, mock-only result, definite pre-acceptance failure, unknown phase and crash before/after durable attempt start, lost final recording, stale owner, repeated synchronization and manual replay. Fake IMAP tests need folder unavailable, duplicate or wrong mailbox Message-ID, UIDVALIDITY reset, DSN success/failure/delay, unsupported DSN and ambiguous/autogenerated evidence. Add API/UI evidence labels and readiness of old rows. Do not rely on Message-ID as an SMTP idempotency key. It is a correlation key.
