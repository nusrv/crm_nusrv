# Owner approval gates

The approved prospective workflow and its prerequisites can proceed independently after implementation authorization. The decisions below apply only to their affected slices.

| Gate | Recommended choice and question for the owner | Affected slice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical official invoice association | Permit ADMIN to associate an existing, demonstrably official invoice to an accepted, unfulfilled legacy renewal through the guarded procedure in the technical design. Approve one invoice counted toward a single case, and one qualifying historical link per case. Require positive evidence for the same customer, issuer, subscription and cycle and exclude ambiguous or previously linked records. May this workflow be activated under those checks? | Activation only. Design, dry-run candidate reporting and prospective association can proceed. |
| Financial exceptions outside ordinary receipt correction | For a genuine payment whose receipt must be removed, replace or correct its receipt atomically when the destination is established. Where keeping real cash would require an unallocated credit, refund, cross-invoice transfer or cancelled-invoice adjustment, surface it for review and preserve existing facts. If such a case appears in the legacy inventory or operational queue, which disposition is authorized? Escalate actual overpayments for explicit treatment if ordinary invoice attribution cannot resolve them. | Only disposition or cutover of affected records. Routine receipt edit, void of erroneous entries, partial/full receipts and verified balances remain in scope. No ordinary overpayment prohibition or automatic credit/refund policy is inferred. |
| Hard-bounce workflow, conditional on changing the case state | Recommend preserving Waiting for Customer and the human renewal decision, showing the bounce and opening a follow-up task for ADMIN. If a hard bounce should automatically reopen or otherwise move a case, which guarded transition is authorized? Until a different transition is approved, apply evidence and follow-up only. | Only a change to case-state policy. Bounce correlation, display and follow-up can ship. |

The separate INDIVIDUAL/PERSONAL design requires an approved visual reference. Its absence blocks that template's implementation and activation only. COMPANY work and preserved historical output can proceed. Implementation itself requires a separate owner authorization.
