# Final technical design

## Service boundaries and authorization

Retain the NestJS modular monolith, Prisma/MariaDB, Next.js and Redis/BullMQ. Keep invoice, collection, renewal, identity and communications logic in domain services. Controllers delegate and the UI reflects server eligibility. Use transactional compare-and-set or an established lock order for state transitions, with an audit event in the same commit. Keep SMTP and mailbox calls outside database transactions. The durable outbox and evidence records survive BullMQ retries and restarts.

Require an individually identified ADMIN for sensitive mutations, including renewal decisions/repair/fulfillment, recipient selection and send, resend authorization, shared review writes, invoice officialization or historical association, QR correction, payments/receipts and integration settings. Enforce an explicit maximum 15-minute access lifetime at configuration/signing and verification. Bind new access credentials to the authenticated session. Resolve current account, role and session state on sensitive actions, and fence writes against concurrent revocation where required. Legacy access tokens without the requisite session binding must refresh or reauthenticate before a sensitive action. Keep legacy roles and users intact and report rollout access differences. Worker executions use a service identity, reference the recorded actor and enforce the persisted command constraints. No worker inherits broad ADMIN authority.

Use the HttpOnly refresh cookie with production Secure, appropriate SameSite, request-origin protections and matching issuance/clearing paths. Enforce a server-side absolute 24-hour refresh session maximum, bounded by earlier configured expiry and revocation. Add guarded browser startup restoration and single-flight refresh with one controlled retry after pre-handler authentication rejection. Preserve idempotency keys for protected mutations. Handle logout racing with refresh so a late response cannot restore authority. Preserve revoked or disabled access restrictions across browser tabs and restarts. Keep token contents out of browser script storage and logs.

Keep AI settings visible and inactive. Close enqueue, routing, provider call, diagnostic and worker/recovery entry points under a server-owned operational gate, including already queued jobs. Preserve prior evidence and human processing. No provider test call from an inactive diagnostic.

## Invoices and renewals

- **Prospective renewal.** After recorded human customer approval, ADMIN can create a linked draft explicitly. Save persists draft changes only. Use a distinct source linkage with checked case/subscription/customer/issuer identity and uniqueness for the qualifying official association. Keep the free/manual path with nullable customer or subscription links where the approved one-time customer flow needs them and sufficient legal snapshots. Keep identifiable customer balances separate from unattached one-time buyers.
- **Officialize Invoice.** ADMIN records details from the actual externally official invoice via **تثبيت الفاتورة الرسمية / Officialize Invoice**. Transactionally lock the invoice, check state/revision, issuer-scoped official-number uniqueness, official date, official totals/currency and validated QR, and freeze legal, financial and presentation data plus provenance. Preserve original CRM values and authoritative official values where reconciliation applies. Audit the separate command, make identical retries safe, and prevent implicit officialization by Save, a background job or a case transition. Existing Fawtara states are compatibility inputs. Define a single verified-official predicate requiring reliable official evidence and effective official status. DRAFT, READY, rejected, cancelled, ambiguous and incomplete legacy records fail it. Use the predicate for payment recording, receipts and fulfillment. Internal line-total mismatch flags alone do not add a fulfillment gate. Remediation stays explicit.
- **Mark Renewal Fulfilled.** Recheck accepted case, authoritative linked official invoice and same subscription/customer/issuer, recorded proposed dates and current subscription tracking date in one transaction. Commit the proposed tracking dates with owned workflow transition and audit. Concurrent fulfillment, changed tracking dates and overtaken or conflicting cycles use explicit conflict handling. No payment or technical adapter call. Legacy missing dates may be entered by ADMIN with reason and validated against case timeline and competing cycles. Date repair alone leaves subscription dates and fulfillment untouched.
- **Historical association, activation gated.** In dry-run, match a demonstrably official invoice to an accepted, unfulfilled legacy case. Require immutable customer and issuer identities, explicit subscription and renewal-cycle evidence (line/service and coverage, or reliable dated original references), no newer/conflicting cycle, no prior qualifying link on either record, and recorded supporting evidence plus reason. Reject unresolved customer/issuer IDs, number-only or amount-only matching, snapshot contradictions, voided/cancelled invoices, and constraints violated by concurrent writes. Lock the case and invoice in a consistent order, enforce uniqueness through a guarded association, and record before/after audit with stable invoice identifiers. Never rewrite the historical invoice, amounts, documents or dates during association. Fulfillment is a later explicit command. Activate only after owner approval.
- **PDF and QR.** Use the official snapshot, including the issuer tax number in the Sales Tax No. slot, and remove the extra conflicting issuer Tax/National line. Preserve bilingual identity, A4 layout, official values, QR, banking and footer from the approved reference and separate draft indication. Keep payment status/history out of invoice PDFs. Validate strict bounded image inputs and content-type agreement, PNG/JPEG/WebP container/marker/chunk integrity and termination, dimensions and bounded image decoding where needed. Reject unsupported, malformed, oversized and deceptive files. QR content decoding is unnecessary. For historic QR corrections append evidence and the replacement asset with reason, actor and timestamp, keeping the original artifact accessible.
- **Issuer presentation.** Select via legal issuer type and freeze a versioned template, issuer names, logo asset, branding, tax/legal/bank details and necessary rendering data at officialization. Use immutable artifact or versioned assets for reproducibility. COMPANY uses the same NUSRV layout for all issuers, including Future Foresight. Preserve available historical outputs and existing snapshots. Do not reconstruct missing branding from current issuer data. New individual template activation awaits the separate approved reference. Preserve access to existing historical individual output.

## Payment and receipt consistency

The working tree adds an invoice-scoped immutable Payment fact and a one-to-one PaymentReceipt with a JSON snapshot. Existing code sums original Payment amounts, uses a separate receipt transaction and permits ACCOUNTANT. Current SQL history does not establish those tables. Treat deployed presence, shapes and contents as unverified until release preparation.

1. Keep immutable Payment facts with invoice, original amount/currency, payment date, method, provenance, actor and scoped idempotency key. Preserve stable Receipt ID and one receipt identity per Payment. Add append-only typed ReceiptRevision records with version, effective decimal amount/currency, state, rendered snapshot/reference, actor, reason and timestamp. Include previous revision and any superseding evidence reference. A unique revision key and guarded current pointer make one current revision effective per receipt. Preserve original JSON and original document when present. No cascading deletes.
2. For the new workflow, in one invoice-serialized transaction, verify official status and current ADMIN, record the actual Payment first, create the receipt and its initial revision, update the effective pointer and write audit. Invoice and receipt display the official invoice number where appropriate. Share that number across receipts and use internal identities for deduplication and audit. A legacy confirmed Payment with no receipt retains its identity and can receive its first receipt after explicit verification. Issuance of its receipt adds no second Payment fact.
3. Edit appends a replacement revision under the original receipt ID. Delete from the operator's effective list by appending an audited void tombstone, retaining all historical facts and versions. Metadata/document correction for genuine cash preserves the applicable amount; a verified erroneous amount can be corrected on the same receipt with evidence. If an erroneous receipt is voided and an established valid replacement is required for real cash, do it atomically against the same payment identity. No implied refund or reassignment. Preserve unexplained cash separately for review where a valid allocation is unknown.
4. Compute invoice outstanding as authoritative official total minus the sum of the current active receipt revisions in the invoice currency. Sum verified outstanding by customer and currency, keeping currencies separate. Derive displayed effective collection and outstanding from the same query or projection and show unresolved records explicitly. Original Payment totals serve audit and reconciliation, contribute zero independently to the balance, and must be explainable by current revision, void, pending issuance or a tracked exception. Use exact database decimals/fixed precision, enforce valid money and currency, and present any excess distinctly without silently clamping or allocating it. Do not infer refunds or customer credit policy.
5. Serialize all related mutations against the invoice, use an expected receipt revision, validate linking and currency on the server, and keep scoped idempotency with canonical payload comparison in the transaction. The same request yields the original outcome; reuse for different input conflicts. Concurrent edits or voids cannot overwrite a newer revision. Apply the same canonical calculation to summaries and exports. Audit before/after effective amounts and financial actor.
6. Inventory legacy invoice states, official evidence, payment and receipt shapes, missing/duplicate/wrong-currency rows, unreceipted facts and mismatches before introducing a new balance. Backfill only reliably paired active receipts, preserving IDs and artifacts and with an idempotent source fingerprint. Keep ambiguous rows as exceptions without guessed approvals, values or reassignment. Reconcile both counts and exact monetary totals by invoice, issuer and currency and make differences visible before cutover. Routine receipts and supported corrections proceed independently of exceptional dispositions.

## Human communications and evidence

- **Recipient and review.** Resolve active BILLING, then active PRIMARY, then valid customer primary email. Reuse normal contact/source rules, including normalized channels and legacy scalar fallback rules. Explicit choice sends a registered record identity, with membership, usability and address checked at authorization and revalidated before dispatch. A changed or removed address stops the queued attempt for review. Persist recipient, content and case snapshots. A human Send bypasses automation Holds only. Suppress forbidden automatic customer reminder creation and dispatch, retaining allowed internal actions. Track shared message read/unread with audit and viewer identity. Opening a case is not blanket review, and new replies are unread. Keep in Review keeps the item available without producing mail or changing its decision.
- **Attempts and dispatch.** Add a durable attempt per logical message, with unique attempt ID, Message-ID, mailbox/configuration provenance, validated envelope recipient, case/purpose/revision, authorization reference, optional linked earlier attempt, attempt boundary, timestamps and sanitized outcome evidence. Claim with a fenced lease. Persist attempt-start/dispatch boundary before network handoff. Capture final positive SMTP response for the exact intended recipient, response timestamp and provider queue ID when available. Distinguish known nonacceptance (pre-handoff or definitive rejection), accepted, and Uncertain (post-boundary disconnect/timeout or crash without proof). Append late evidence even when the worker has lost the lease. An old worker cannot directly change the workflow after losing ownership.
- **Retries and uncertain resend.** Automatically retry only proven retryable nonacceptance or work proven unstarted under the new contract, with bounded backoff. A stale started attempt lacking trustworthy evidence becomes Uncertain. Treat ambiguous legacy in-flight attempts conservatively. Stable Message-ID provides correlation, independently of provider deduplication. Both reminder and reply outboxes must enforce this before any wider scheduling or stale reclaim. ADMIN sees uncertainty and duplicate risk and can authorize a new linked, uniquely correlated attempt via an idempotent action. Recheck latest evidence and existing authorizations at commit and again before dispatch. Surface late acceptance of the original and prevent unsent duplicate dispatch when discovered; if both have already left CRM retain both outcomes. Never retry uncertainty as a normal queue operation.
- **Active Email Sync.** Continue existing inbound sync, add available Sent-folder and supported provider evidence adapters for all configured message streams, including general/inbound mail, operator replies, renewal messages and invoice/application outbound streams where supported. Account for disabled/unsupported mailboxes and historical gaps visibly. Scope checkpoints by configuration, mailbox/folder and UIDVALIDITY. Deduplicate source objects/events, preserve checkpoint and boundary consistency, and handle UID epoch resets with bounded replay or review. Preserve case-sensitive identifier correlation while checking mailbox, intended recipient and stable provider/envelope identifiers where available. Only positive trustworthy evidence can change derived status. Sent copy means observed in Sent, and missing copy or legacy DELIVERED does not prove acceptance, delivery or failure. Preserve conflicting/unmatched evidence and surface Action/Error Queue items. Do not alter mailbox flags or backfill Sent copies as proof.
- **Clarification and bounce.** On actual positive SMTP acceptance of the exact case-linked clarification purpose/revision, append acceptance and transactionally project Waiting for Customer with a guarded, idempotent transition. Direct Waiting routes cannot bypass the gate for a case in Needs Clarification. A newer review, decision or superseding clarification prevents stale evidence from acting on current workflow. Monitor DSNs before ordinary inbound reply/unread classification. Correlate trusted mailbox/provider evidence to the correct attempt and recipient; parser shape alone is insufficient. Hard bounce updates the delivery outcome and opens a deduplicated Action Queue follow-up, preserving prior acceptance and the current renewal decision and Waiting state. Soft or ambiguous evidence stays labeled as such. ADMIN can review, contact or send intentionally under the approved safeguards. Any new bounce-driven case transition is gated on owner policy.
