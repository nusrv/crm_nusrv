# Implementation acceptance tests

These are planned release criteria. This documentation task ran no application tests or migrations. Use isolated MariaDB fixtures, mock adapters and controlled SMTP/IMAP harnesses after implementation authorization. Verify backend and UI routes, persistent state and audit, including retries and concurrent commands.

| Area | Testable acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Authorization (OD-2, CL-3) | Configure access >15 minutes and prove effective maximum ≤15 minutes. Reuse an otherwise valid token after role removal, disablement or session revocation and confirm sensitive reads/writes are denied before side effects. Test every sensitive endpoint and worker command boundary. Two ADMINs produce distinct actors. Legacy ACCOUNTANT/MANAGER without ADMIN cannot perform collection. Old tokens cannot bypass session binding. |
| Session (OD-9) | Restart the browser with valid refresh and no access cookie and restore the protected route. Expired, revoked and disabled refresh fails. Config >24h cannot extend server validity. Concurrent 401s restore once per browser context and retry safely at most once. Logout clears correct cookies, revokes authority and wins a refresh race including across tabs. Test cookie flags, trusted origin and mutation idempotency. |
| Renewal invoice (OD-3, CL-4) | Unapproved or rejected cases cannot create renewal invoices. Approval creates no invoice automatically. ADMIN manually selects linked draft and enters number, date, official values and QR. Draft Save and ready state cannot officialize. Only explicit labeled action officializes. Reject missing/invalid evidence, wrong customer/issuer/case and issuer-scoped duplicate number. Concurrent officialization produces one result and audit. Free invoice including one-time buyer works without artificial case/subscription. |
| Fulfillment and recovery (OD-3, OD-7, CL-4) | Reject draft, incomplete legacy accepted, wrong association and conflict with newer cycle or tracking date. Valid linked unpaid or paid official invoice qualifies. Authorized action updates CRM tracking dates exactly once with audit and no external integration calls. Legacy date correction requires reason, leaves subscription and status intact, and never guesses dates. |
| Historical association (CL-4, gated) | With feature disabled, all attachment mutations fail. After owner approval, positive historical customer, issuer, case/subscription/cycle and invoice provenance permit an audited link with no change to official invoice data or dates. Test conflicting/missing evidence, prior links, fulfilled/rejected cases and simultaneous competing associations. Association does not fulfill the case. |
| Payment and receipt (OD-4, CL-1/2/3) | Reject payment or receipt against draft, READY, void/cancelled or unverified official record, even by direct API. Reject a receipt without actual payment. Record real partial or full payment and initial receipt atomically. Failure between inserts rolls back both. For invoice 100.000, receipts 40.000 and 30.000 yield 30.000 due. Revise 40.000 to 35.000 and see 35.000 due. Void the 30.000 receipt and see 65.000 due. Original facts and all receipt versions survive. Both receipts show the same official number with distinct internal identities. |
| Collection integrity (CL-1/2) | Same-key same-payload replay returns the original even after further balance changes. Same key with different payload conflicts. Simultaneous record/edit/void and stale version tests prove one active revision and no lost updates or duplicated payments. Existing real unreceipted payment may receive a first receipt without a new Payment. Fixture tests verify precise decimals, multi-currency separation, unattached buyers, mismatch quarantine, visible excess and reconciled API/UI/export totals. No raw ledger contribution is added to receipt totals. Confirm wrong invoice/currency and unauthorized role are denied. |
| QR (OD-8) | Valid bounded PNG, JPEG and WebP fixtures with ordinary image content pass structural checks without QR decoding. Reject mismatched MIME, forged headers, truncation, corrupt length/segment/chunk boundaries, invalid base64, unsafe dimensions or decoded size, unsupported formats and active content. Historical correction retains original bytes and doc metadata with actor/time/reason and replacement pointer. |
| Issuer templates (OD-11, CL-7) | COMPANY NUSRV, Future Foresight and another company render the common approved layout with their correct bilingual legal names, logo, tax number, QR and bank/footer data. Sales Tax No. comes from issuer snapshot and no duplicate issuer Tax/National line appears. Draft is distinct, PDF omits payment history/status and official values are preserved. Editing current issuer/branding/template cannot change historical output. Individual template is unavailable until its approved reference exists, and historical output remains available. |
| Recipient and manual send (OD-1, OD-6) | Confirm active BILLING → active PRIMARY → primary email priority and deterministic tie handling. Select a valid alternate registered contact. Reject arbitrary, inactive, cross-customer and changed/removed addresses at appropriate stages. Human Send bypasses related automation Holds while retaining decision and recipient guardrails. Scheduled/legacy jobs cannot dispatch automatic customer renewal reminders. Internal allowed paths remain functional. |
| Review and clarification (OD-5, CL-6) | New inbound message is unread for all. Explicit review is shared and audited, opening unrelated views leaves state intact. Keep in Review sends nothing. Draft, queue, attempt, Sent copy, mock outside test and ambiguous SMTP response cannot advance. Positive acceptance for the correct recipient, case and clarification version advances once. Generic direct Waiting and stale acceptance after a newer decision cannot override the gate. Positive acceptance does not require mailbox delivery. |
| Attempts and resends (OD-12, CL-5) | Simulate disconnect after DATA, worker death after SMTP acceptance before CRM commit, lease expiry, concurrent polls, delayed original evidence and existing ambiguous stale rows across both outboxes. No automatic second transmission when outcome is uncertain. Bounded retries apply only to proven nonacceptance or unstarted work. With 50 delayed rows and an eligible newer row, the newer row is processed and scheduledAt remains intact. Warn before human resend, store ADMIN and original link, reject duplicate authorization, and suppress an unsent resend when late original acceptance arrives. Both accepted attempts remain evidenced if already sent. |
| Active Sync and bounce (OD-12, CL-5/6) | Exercise all supported inbound/outbound sources, optional Sent/provider capabilities and unavailable folders. Verify per-folder cursors, UIDVALIDITY change, duplicate/out-of-order events, concurrent sync and worker restart. Sent observation and legacy DELIVERED never imply final delivery. Correlated hard bounce updates delivery and opens one follow-up without changing Waiting/decision or emitting retry. Untrusted/ambiguous or conflicting DSNs are retained for review and do not misclassify as human replies. |
| AI (OD-10) | Settings display inactive. Existing credentials, old enable flags, direct calls, queue/recovery and diagnostic actions cannot execute provider calls or automated routing. Human workflow and historical evidence remain available. |
| Migration and release | Compare baseline schema/history and representative dirty/legacy records. Fresh and upgrade dry-run on disposable copy. Repeat backfill and compare IDs, source fingerprints, counts and exact currency totals. Verify rollback does not delete history or restore an unsafe old worker. Preserve legacy account roles and financial evidence. Regression covers existing modals, threading, manual/free invoicing and audit. |
