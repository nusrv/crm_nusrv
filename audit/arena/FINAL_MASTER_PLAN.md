### A-03 agreed design
Add nullable, indexed `CommunicationOutbox.nextAttemptAt`. Selection and atomic claim share `scheduledAt<=now AND ((QUEUED AND (nextAttemptAt IS NULL OR nextAttemptAt<=now)) OR (PROCESSING AND lastAttemptAt<staleBefore))`; the claim also matches `id`. Order queued rows by COALESCE(nextAttemptAt,scheduledAt), stale PROCESSING rows by lease expiry, tie-break `id`. Preserve `scheduledAt` and lease ownership. Set `nextAttemptAt` on every requeue incl. bounded SMTP retries; defer consumes zero SMTP attempts. Verify: 50 blocked + 1 newer eligible, late poll makes all 51 due, newer row still processed; early claim rejected; stale lease recoverable; delivery after hold release. (My no-DDL `updatedAt` alternative was rejected: it fails when blocked rows all become due before a poll. A retry interval longer than the poll period is not a correctness requirement.)
