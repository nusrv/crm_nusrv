# OWNER DECISIONS — APPROVED (recorded as given by the project owner; meaning unaltered)

Authority: explicit owner instruction (priority 1 in the source-of-truth hierarchy; it outranks `CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SCOPE.md` where they differ). Nothing here is a recommendation of Claude or Codex. No implementation is authorized by this file.

| ID | Decision | Approved policy |
|---|---|---|
| OD-1 | **Option C** | A human operator may choose a reminder recipient **only from the customer's registered contacts**. An unregistered address must first be added to the customer's contacts. Default resolution order unchanged: active BILLING → active PRIMARY → authoritative primary customer email fallback. TECHNICAL/MANAGEMENT never silently selected; an operator may explicitly pick another registered contact. Keep contact validation, communication history and auditability. **No free-text recipient override.** |
| OD-2 | **Option C** | Access tokens may live up to 15 minutes. **Sensitive operations perform a current authorization check before execution**: account still enabled; current roles/permissions authorize the action; applicable session-revocation respected. Sensitive = financial actions, payment & receipt changes, user/role administration, official invoice recording, other appropriately identified privileged operations. Claude and Codex must identify and justify the complete protected endpoint/action inventory. **No DB check on every ordinary read.** Preserve silent refresh, logout revocation, disabled-user restrictions, current-role refresh checks, approved session duration. |
| OD-3 | **Option C** | A renewal must have an **officially recorded invoice** before a human may run **Mark Renewal Fulfilled**. A draft is insufficient. **Enforced by the backend**, not only the UI. Payment is completely independent: an official invoice may be unpaid/partial/paid; payment status never affects fulfilment eligibility. Fulfil is CRM tracking only, never triggers external renewal; only an authorized human action commits the new tracking renewal date. |
| OD-4 | **Custom** | **A. Payment recording** must not depend on the invoice being officially issued inside the CRM; real-world payment is independent of officialization. The capability to **issue a customer payment receipt** activates only after the corresponding official invoice has been created or officially recorded (not permission to issue a receipt before one exists). **B. Receipt number**: the receipt displays the **same invoice number** as the official invoice (per approved receipt reference); no separate customer-visible receipt sequence without owner approval; several payments/receipts per invoice keep unique internal ids. **C. Correction/deletion**: the CRM operator is the manager; the manager must be able to **modify or delete an incorrect payment receipt** (approved requirement), preserving financial integrity, traceability and audit. **Owner flagged as still unclarified:** whether payments may exist before any CRM invoice record exists, and how deleting a receipt affects its underlying payment record. |
| OD-5 | **B / K2 / U1** | **Needs Clarification (B):** support preparing and reviewing a clarification email; case moves to **Waiting for Customer only after the email has actually been sent** through the human-send workflow. A draft or unsent queued message must not establish that a request was sent; if sending fails the CRM must not represent the customer as having received it. **Keep in Review (K2):** keeps case under human review; marks the reply read; does not approve/reject; does not close; keeps the pending decision visible. **Unread (U1):** read/unread is **shared** across authorized users; keep review/audit trail; clearly distinguish "message read" from "case still awaiting human decision". |
| OD-6 | **Option B** | An explicit human-authorized send **bypasses an automation Hold**. Holds apply to automated operations, not a deliberate manual Send. Do not silently defer a manually approved email solely due to a Hold. Sending remains an explicit user action. **Does not authorize automatic reminders**; the prohibition remains. |
| OD-7 | **Option B** | For historical accepted cases lacking a proposed period, the **manager enters** the renewal period, start/end dates and new proposed tracking date, reviews, and **documents the reason**. No automatic mass reconstruction; no silent change of subscription renewal dates. First do a **read-only inventory** of affected cases where an authorized data source exists. Final tracking date still committed only by human Mark Renewal Fulfilled; OD-3 invoice prerequisite still applies. |
| OD-8 | **B / S1 / H3** | **Formats (B):** accept PNG, JPEG, WebP with **structural validation**; owner accepts structural checks don't guarantee full JPEG/WebP decode integrity; do not silently narrow to PNG-only; add practical safeguards against malformed input and resource use; document residual limits. **Verification (S1):** structural only; **no mandatory QR-symbol decoding or external authenticity service**; manager checks the image against the externally issued invoice; do not claim structural validation proves an authentic/readable QR. **Historical (H3):** manager may replace a suspicious historical QR after verification; preserve original image/immutable evidence reference, replacement, who, when, correction history; preserve official invoice identity and financial integrity; no automatic rewrite of historical records. |
| OD-9 | **Option A** | After closing/reopening the browser the session is recovered automatically while the refresh session is valid; effective duration ≤ 24 h; logout, disablement and expired refresh prevent recovery; keep short-lived access tokens with secure silent refresh; **no 24-hour privileged access token**; compatible with OD-2. |
| OD-10 | **Option A** | AI settings stay visible under **Advanced / System Settings**, labelled **"Deferred — Currently Inactive"**; AI architecture may remain; AI must not participate in operational workflows (no reply classification, content generation, renewal decisions, invoice creation, payment decisions, etc.); **no live external AI calls** in this reconciliation; historical AI jobs must not keep mutating operational workflow state. |
| OD-11 | **Option C** | **Separate approved invoice templates per legal issuer type** (Company, Individual/Personal), based on approved visual references; not necessarily one per billing entity (same-type entities may share). Legal issuer remains `BillingEntity`. Preserve issuer identity, tax/legal fields, branding & invoice snapshots, issuer isolation, official values. NUSRV reference stays authoritative for its layout. Sales Tax No. = issuer's tax number, never the customer's. No unapproved tax/identity lines. Identify missing references and issuer-specific compliance questions rather than inventing layouts. |
| OD-12 | **Custom** | **Mandatory** effective **active synchronization** to verify and maintain the status of every email. Design the strongest technically realistic solution on the existing mail architecture, using SMTP acceptance/response, outbox state/attempts, IMAP Sent-folder reconciliation where supported, Message-ID/thread correlation, DSN/failure/bounce evidence, retry/interruption/uncertain handling. Distinguish (conceptually, not necessarily as a DB enum): Prepared, Queued, Sending, Accepted by SMTP, Confirmed in Sent (if observable), Delivery confirmed (trustworthy evidence only), Failed/Bounced, Uncertain. SMTP acceptance, Sent appearance, mailbox delivery and human reading are different events; do not claim universal proof. **No automatic resend of uncertain customer reminders without explicit owner approval**; return a justified proposed policy for uncertain outcomes. |
